How to mark (read this first)
  1. Read the passage (on the right; below on a phone). For this task the passage is the only truth.
  2. Read the question. A small AI model was given this passage and this question.
  3. Read the model's answer (the box marked ANSWER) and check every claim in it against the passage.
  4. If some words in the answer say something the passage does not say, or say it differently from the passage, select those words in the answer with the mouse. Then press the button that says what is wrong.
  5. When you have checked the whole answer, press the green Finish button. If you marked nothing, it says “No errors: finish this answer”. The page then shows the next answer.

You only select words in the answer. The passage and the question are only for reading; they cannot be marked.

Example from these answers: the passage says “more than 95%”, the answer says “approximately 95%”. Select “approximately 95%” and press Subtle conflict.

The four types in more detail
Evident conflictThe passage clearly says something different: a wrong number, name, step or the opposite claim.
Subtle conflictClose to the passage, but the meaning is changed: a weaker or stronger word, a changed condition or scope (“approximately 95%” for “more than 95%”).
Evident baselessA clear claim the passage does not contain at all, even if it is true in the world.
Subtle baselessA small added detail, reason, generalisation or inference that is not in the passage.

Mark the shortest part that carries the wrong claim. Do not mark: rewording of the passage, “Based on the passage…”, the question repeated, the reply “Unable to answer based on given passages.”, a true statement that the passage does not say something, missing information, general advice, or formatting.
