How to mark (short guide)
Evident conflictSubtle conflictEvident baselessSubtle baseless
Evident conflictThe passage clearly contradicts it: a wrong number, name, step or the opposite claim.
Subtle conflictSlightly changes the passage's meaning: a weaker or stronger word, a changed condition or scope ("approximately 95%" for "more than 95%").
Evident baselessA clear claim the passage does not contain, even if it is true in the world.
Subtle baselessA small added detail, reason, generalisation or inference.

Mark the shortest part that carries the claim. Do not mark paraphrases, "Based on the passage…", repeated questions, the reply "Unable to answer based on given passages.", true statements that the passage does not say something, missing information, generic advice, or formatting.
